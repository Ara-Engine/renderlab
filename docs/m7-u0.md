# M7-U0: Unreal configurations, reference renderer, glossy cause, benches

Unit M7-U0 of [the M7 design](m7-design.md) (the research's G0, [gi.md](research/gi.md#units)), 2026-11-07, branch `t3/m7-u0` on main `7e00a32`. Contract: [milestone-7.md](milestone-7.md). Labels: **M** measured here, **A** author-reported elsewhere, **I** inference.

## Summary

- **Unreal configuration study: done, with per-run engine readback.**
  - Lumen software Epic, the non-RT floor, costs **+0.92 ms** over TSR on the quarry at 1080p. GI is 1.21 ms and reflections 0.07 ms (async off), and LocalUsed is **5,142 MiB**.
  - Lumen hardware Epic, the RT floor, costs **+1.51 ms** over TSR with HRT shadows. GI is 1.24 ms and reflections 0.07 ms, and LocalUsed is **6,444 MiB**.
  - **TSR alone costs 1.41-1.59 ms.**
  - Hit lighting adds 0.15 ms of reflections at Epic. Cinematic costs about 3x Epic's GI.
  - Every one of the 64 runs is proven by `DumpCVars` (value and SetBy) against the requested settings and the installed `BaseScalability.ini` groups. Its effective tracing mode (software, hardware, hit lighting) is read from the engine's own pass names.
  - A harness artifact that blacked out every Lumen frame (pre-exposure override vs Lumen's ray-intensity clamps) was found and fixed before the numbers above.
- **The 84.75% glossy disagreement is explained and closed on the matched RHI.**
  - It came from Unreal's Vulkan SM5 relaxed precision: the BxDF N·H sits on the fp16 grid.
  - On D3D12 SM6 the same fixture agrees with the physical reference to 0.23% at roughness 0.1.
  - Separately, both engines' sharp sun highlights are up to about 40% from an exactly integrated 4.58° sun disk at roughness 0.1. That is a requirement for U1/U4.
- **Reference renderer: built and validated.**
  - `renderlab::reference` is an in-process GPU path tracer (sun disk, sky, multi-bounce, pixel- and shutter-integrated).
  - It passes analytic gates and an independent Mitsuba 3 cross-check on two scenes (global bias ≤0.005%, block z RMS 0.83-0.95). A same-seed replay is bit-identical.
  - **The quarry reference itself is a miss:** textures and the prepared sky are not yet supported.
- **Four new benches** (SV, S4, S5, S2G), plus two cross-check scenes, are generated for both renderers (JSON + glTF + Mitsuba) and have first references. They are not yet imported into Unreal.


## 1. Unreal 5.7.4 configuration study (U0a)

### Method

- **Build and content.** Packaged Development UE 5.7.4 CL51494982, D3D12 SM6, `-RenderOffscreen`, hidden process, no window, no focus change or input automation. The packaged executable is byte-identical to the e2e one (SHA256 `69d6f88a…a6e5`).
- **Recook with distance fields.** Software Lumen needs mesh distance fields and cards, which the matched package never had. The project was copied to `E:/renderlab-work/m7-u0/ue-project` (its own DDC) with `r.GenerateMeshDistanceFields=True`, `r.DistanceFields.SupportEvenIfHardwareRayTracingSupported=1` and `r.PathTracing=True`, then cooked (97 s) and paked (10.5 s) to a new package. The shared quarry package that other workers use is untouched. The new content identity is the pak `a5cea43d…5c645` and utoc `8d53240f…1d0e`; the config is `371d0f4d…8356`.
- **Distance-field readback.** `r.DistanceFields.LogAtlasStats 2` in the running game lists all twelve scans: an 8 MB atlas, mip-0 resolutions from 21×21×14 (Quarry_04) to 84×91×35 (Quarry_11) at the default voxel density and resolution scale 1 (M). `r.LumenScene.DumpStats 1` reads 12 mesh cards and 66 cards (M).
- **Harness.** This is the matched e2e workload: 1920×1080 internal and output, the registered twelve scans, sun, environment and manual −14 EV, VSM SMRT 8×8, Nanite RT mode 1. Native CSV `GPUTime` frames 96-159 (n=64) give the async-on frame. ProfileGPU at frame 64 runs with `r.RDG.AsyncCompute 0`, `r.Lumen.AsyncCompute 0` and `r.RDG.Events 3` for pass attribution and tracing-mode evidence. All three are restored before the timed block; both async defaults are 1 in the engine source.
- **Workloads.** Static is pose 0. Camera alternates 1 cm every frame and sun alternates 1°, as in e2e. Pan moves the camera 2 cm per frame from frame 96, with captures at 97/112/128/159. The sun step is a +10° yaw at frame 24, and the yaw step is the sky cubemap angle +10° followed by `r.SkylightRecapture`; each is captured at +1, 2, 4 … 128 frames.
- **Readback proof, per run.** `DumpCVars -csv` runs at frames 40 and 95 and records every CVar with its value and SetBy. The runner checks the configuration's explicit settings and every value that its scalability groups set in the installed `BaseScalability.ini` (`GlobalIlluminationQuality@N`, `ReflectionQuality@N`, `AntiAliasingQuality@3`). It also checks that nothing drifted between frames 40 and 95. **The effective tracing mode comes from the engine's own RDG pass names in the ProfileGPU frame**, not from the requested CVars. Software Lumen shows `DistanceFieldTracing … probes` and `TraceVoxels`, hardware shows `HardwareRayTracingCS …` and `ReflectionHardwareRayTracingRGS default`, and hit lighting adds `ReflectionHardwareRayTracingRGS hit-lighting` (M).
- **Two recorded exceptions.** The matched project's `[SystemSettings]` sets `r.DistanceFieldAO=0` and `r.SSR.Quality=0`, which outrank the Epic group's 1 and 3. Both are inactive in these configurations: Lumen GI replaces DFAO, and SSR runs only when `r.ReflectionMethod` is 2.

### A harness artifact found and fixed

The first full study (`study/a`) ran the e2e command set unchanged, including `r.EyeAdaptation.PreExposureOverride 1`. Every configuration with Lumen reflections captured an almost black frame: mean 0.03/255 against 12.37 for UE-0. GI-only came out at 3.8/255.

- **Cause.** The override keeps scene colour unexposed, so values sit in the thousands under −14 EV. Lumen's firefly clamps are written for pre-exposed values (`r.Lumen.ScreenProbeGather.MaxRayIntensity` 10, `r.Lumen.Reflections.MaxRayIntensity` 40, `r.LumenScene.Radiosity.MaxRayIntensity` 40, read back), so they clipped almost all traced light. Most of this scene's light comes from the sky cubemap (sky light intensity 64, with a bright sun region), not from the 1,000 lux directional light.
- **Fix.** All reported runs use the engine's own manual-exposure pre-exposure (override 0, read back). A smoke lease confirmed bright Lumen frames before the full rerun (12.1 and 12.0/255).
- **Status of study/a.** It is retained as e2e-identical evidence for the non-Lumen baselines only. Its Lumen numbers are not used.

## 2. Reference renderer (U0b)

**`renderlab::reference`** (`src/reference.rs`, `shaders/reference_path_tracer.wgsl`) is an in-process GPU path tracer on the RT device. It uses wgpu ray queries in one compute pass, one BLAS per mesh and one TLAS.

**What it estimates.**
- The physical image under a sun disk (uniform radiance; a delta sun when the radius is 0), a constant sky and emissive surfaces.
- Any number of bounces, ended by Russian roulette after the third.
- Each sample draws its own pixel position (box filter over the footprint) and shutter time, interpolating the camera from `open` to `close`. Equal poses give zero shutter.

**Model.**
- **BSDF.** The standard program's: Lambert × (1 − metal) plus GGX with Schlick F0 = 0.04·specular·(1 − metal) + base·metal and α = max(r², 10⁻⁴), with the program's |N×H|² NDF form. Smith masking is height-correlated, as in the program. A separable form exists only to match Mitsuba's `roughconductor` in the cross-check.
- **Surfaces** are shaded two-sided, and the sun disk hides the sky behind it.

**Estimator.**
- NEE for the sun (uniform cone sampling) and the sky (cosine), BSDF sampling (Heitz VNDF for the GGX lobe, cosine for Lambert), combined by the power heuristic.
- There is no reuse across pixels or frames.
- Each pixel keeps even- and odd-sample sums, so it reports a split-half standard error with its mean.
- Seeds are a stateless PCG hash of pixel, sample and seed, so a same-seed replay is bit-identical.
- A non-finite sample poisons its pixel and is counted, never hidden.

**API.** `reference::render(device, queue, &Scene, &View, &Settings) -> Image` owns its resources for one call and never runs in the frame loop. `reference_tests::load_bench` reads the schema-2 bench JSON.

### Validation (M, lease `reference-a`, 100 s)

**Analytic gates** (`gpu_reference_analytic`). The registered pass condition is |mean − 1| < max(4σ, 0.2%).

| Case | Expected | Relative mean ± σ |
|---|---|---|
| Furnace: closed emissive Lambert box, Le = 2, ρ = 0.75, seen from inside; every bounce to Russian roulette | L = Le/(1−ρ) = 8 | 0.99940 ± 0.00021 |
| Delta sun at 30° elevation on a Lambert floor, black sky | ρ/π·E·cos θ | 1.000000 (zero variance) |
| Constant sky on a Lambert floor | ρ·L_sky | 1.000000 (zero variance) |

The furnace's −0.06% sits at 2.8σ, inside the registered 0.2% but not explained by noise alone. The likely cause is ray-origin offsets (10⁻⁴ m along the geometric normal) placing origins outside the box within 10⁻⁴ m of its edges. It is recorded, not tuned away.

**Determinism.** A same-seed replay of X1 at 32,768 spp is bit-identical (`replay.json`).

**Independent cross-check against Mitsuba 3.9.1** (`scalar_rgb`, CPU, path integrator, `max_depth −1`, box filter, two seeds × 16,384 spp). The scene, camera and BSDFs are written from the same `scene.json`. The tolerance was registered in `scripts/m7-u0-crosscheck.py` before comparing. Our side ran at 32,768 spp with separable Smith, matching Mitsuba's `roughconductor`.

| Scene | Global bias | Bias / σ | Relative L1 (Mitsuba seed-to-seed) | Block z RMS | Blocks beyond 4σ | Pass |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| X1: open box, colour bleeding, F=1 GGX sphere α 0.30, delta sun + sky | +0.0040% | 2.9 | 0.39% (0.68%) | 0.95 | 0.03% | yes |

- **Pass criteria.** The global bias must be under 0.5% and under 4σ. On 8×8 blocks with signal, the normalised difference must have an RMS under 1.5, with fewer than 0.5% of blocks beyond 4σ.
- **Sigma floor.** Block σ has a floor of 10⁻⁵ × the block mean. This was added after the first comparison: directly seen constant sky has zero variance in both renderers and differs only by f32 rounding. It is a measurement correction, not a loosened threshold.
- **Inspection.** The difference images (`X1-front-crosscheck.png`, `X2-front-crosscheck.png`: ours | Mitsuba | 8× |difference|) are structureless noise, with no edge, highlight or penumbra pattern.
- **What this validates.** Transport, multi-bounce, MIS, the sun disk, the sky, the pixel filter, the camera and the GGX lobe. The height-correlated Smith form used on the quarry is a one-line visibility change from the separable form tested here.

## 3. The 84.75% glossy disagreement (U0c)

**Cause: reduced precision in Unreal's Vulkan SM5 shaders, not a convention, exposure, Fresnel or environment-filtering difference, and not a Renderlab bug (M).**

- **The setting.** The retained M5 fixture capture ran UE's editor commandlet with `-vulkan`, which is Vulkan SM5. UE's SPIR-V compiler defines `PLATFORM_SUPPORTS_RELAXED_PRECISION=1` for Vulkan SM5 (`VulkanShaderFormat/Private/SpirVShaderCompiler.inl`, `ModifyCompilerInput`). `Platform.ush` then maps `half` to `min16float`, which becomes SPIR-V `RelaxedPrecision`. UE's `BxDFContext` stores N·L, N·V, V·L, N·H and V·H as `half`, built from `half3` N, V and L (`BRDF.ush`).
- **The effect.** Just below 1, fp16 has a spacing of 2⁻¹¹ = 4.88×10⁻⁴. At roughness 0.1 the GGX lobe is narrower than that: α² ≈ 9.2×10⁻⁵, and half of the lobe lies within 1 − N·H ≲ α²/2. So N·H can take only the values 1, 1 − 2⁻¹¹, 1 − 2⁻¹⁰, …, and the NDF collapses from about 3,400 at the peak to 25.8 or 7.0.
- **On D3D12 SM6**, which the quarry comparisons use, UE defines `PLATFORM_SUPPORTS_REAL_TYPES` only for shaders that opt into `CFLAG_AllowRealTypes` (Lumen screen probe gather, TSR, VSM projection and a few others), not for deferred lighting. There `half` compiles as `float` (`ShaderFormatD3D.cpp`). These are installed-engine source reads, used only to diagnose; nothing was copied.

**Evidence (M, `scripts/m7-u0-glossy.py`, CPU only, the retained probe buffers):**

1. **Implied N·H sits on the fp16 grid.** For each strongly specular pixel, the observed UE specular was inverted for the N·H that the physical NDF needs, with every other factor held at its decoded value. On the roughness-0.1 metal, the 23 pixels' implied 1 − N·H lands on integer multiples of 2⁻¹¹ with a mean distance of **0.0018 steps**. The physical values sit 0.26 steps away, and a continuum would average 0.25. On the roughness-0.1 dielectric, the 28 pixels give 0.0045 against 0.19. The implied levels are 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 steps, never 0: N·H never reaches 1.
2. **Full-frame fp16 emulation explains the residual on rough objects.** The direct BRDF was re-evaluated with N, V, L, the dot products and N·H rounded to fp16. That cuts UE's relative-L1 error on every rough object 3-4x:

   | Object | UE vs physical | UE vs fp16 emulation |
   |---|---:|---:|
   | metal r0.5 | 5.84% | 1.60% |
   | dielectric r0.5 | 0.368% | 0.116% |
   | floor r0.5 | 0.131% | 0.042% |
   | mirrored panel | 0.109% | 0.026% |

   At roughness 0.1 the emulation does not reproduce the exact per-pixel level. Which grid level a pixel lands on depends on the driver's fp16 operation order, which is unknown. The implied-level test above is independent of that order.
3. **Bound.** The quantization dominates when the lobe's width in 1 − N·H, about r⁴/2, is comparable to 2⁻¹¹, that is r ≲ 0.18. By r ≈ 0.3 its effect falls to the 0.1-2% level measured above.

**Control on D3D12 SM6 (M).** The same prepared fixture level and camera were recaptured with `UnrealEditor-Cmd -AllowCommandletRendering -dx12 -sm6` (log: "Using Forced RHI: D3D12 … Feature Level SM6"). The run captured scene HDR, decoded normals and base colour (`scripts/m7-u0-ue-fixture.py`, lease `fixture-d3d12-b`). The scene was then re-scored against the same physical model using D3D12's own normals. Pixels at the RGBA16F target's 65,504 ceiling are excluded (1 pixel at angle 0).

| Object | Vulkan SM5 (retained) | **D3D12 SM6, delta sun** |
|---|---:|---:|
| metal r0.1 | 84.75% (vs Renderlab), 84.25% (vs physical) | **0.228%** |
| dielectric r0.1 | 3.28% | **0.039%** |
| metal r0.5 | 5.84% | 4.00% |
| dielectric r0.5 | 0.368% | 0.285% |

- **No grid on D3D12.** The implied N·H is now continuous. Its mean distance to the 2⁻¹¹ grid is 0.261 on metal and 0.208 on dielectric, the same as the physical values' 0.260 and 0.208. It follows the physical N·H to **0.006 steps RMS**. So the glossy disagreement is explained, and on the matched RHI it is closed to 0.2% at roughness 0.1.
- **Remaining difference.** About 4% remains on the roughness-0.5 metal. This is a bounded model difference: UE uses an approximate joint Smith visibility rather than the exact height-correlated form. Dielectrics differ by 0.3% or less.

**Sun-disk convention (M).** The control was repeated at UE light source angles 0.5357° (UE's default) and 4.584° (the quarry's 0.04 rad radius disk). The exact reference integrates the uniform disk by 32×32 stratified cone quadrature.

| Object | UE vs disk at 0.5357° | UE vs disk at 4.584° | **Delta sun vs disk at 4.584°** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| metal r0.1 | 9.50% | 39.9% | **38.5%** |
| dielectric r0.1 | 0.38% | 1.29% | 1.93% |
| metal r0.5 | 4.05% | 4.45% | 0.23% |
| dielectric r0.5 | 0.29% | 0.32% | 0.04% |

- **Both engines are far off at low roughness.** UE's area-light approximation for the disk is up to 40% from the exact disk on a roughness-0.1 metal. So is the standard program's treatment of the sun as a delta direction for specular (`light_sample` in `shaders/program_api.wgsl`).
- **Consequences.**
  - On the quarry's 4.58° sun, neither engine's sharp highlights are physical below roughness about 0.2.
  - Glossy gates must score against the disk-integrated reference.
  - Disk-integrated sun specular is a requirement for U1/U4: it is the only way to beat UE on glossy, and it is a lighting/shading item that M7 owns.

**Consequences.**

- Glossy comparisons against Unreal must run on D3D12 SM6, the matched quarry RHI, never on the Vulkan SM5 commandlet path.
- Renderlab's BRDF already evaluates the GGX denominator as |N×H|² + α²(N·H)² in f32 (`shaders/scene.wgsl`), which avoids the cancellation even in f32.
- No gain or roughness fit is applied to either engine.

## 5. Patents and licences

- **The reference tracer** is plain unidirectional path tracing:
  - next-event estimation for the sun and sky;
  - BSDF sampling combined by the power heuristic (Veach and Guibas 1995);
  - GGX visible-normal sampling (Heitz 2018);
  - Russian roulette;
  - the PCG hash (Jarzynski and Olano 2020).

  It uses no reservoirs (ReSTIR), world-space or radiance caches, variance clipping, SVGF/gradient estimation or denoising. **No technique flagged in [milestone-7.md](milestone-7.md#patent-policy) is used** in the reference or the tooling.
- **Mitsuba 3.9.1** (BSD-3) and Dr.Jit run as an external tool, installed in an isolated venv on E:. No Mitsuba code is in the repo.
- **Unreal** engine source and configuration files were read to diagnose and configure only. Only setting names appear here.

## 6. Misses and limits

1. **No quarry reference yet (R_GI on the quarry): the largest miss.** The tracer renders the generated benches, but not the quarry. Three pieces are missing:
   - base-colour, metallic-roughness and normal textures (the quarry has 12 × 4K maps);
   - the prepared environment as a sampled sky: the RLE2 field's level 0 is the quarry's unblurred 128² cube, which needs piecewise-constant cube importance sampling for NEE;
   - an RLA upload path for the 13,034,903 source triangles.

   Plan: a texture-array binding sampled at level 0 (footprint-integrated by the pixel jitter itself), a cube CDF, and the existing asset loader in a test harness. Until then, no Unreal configuration can be scored against a reference on the quarry, so UE's quarry error per measure is not yet recorded.
2. **Motion-integrated sequences.**
   - The tracer integrates a camera shutter. Bench movers (S4 door, S5 cube) are static within a frame, so moving-object shutter integration is not implemented.
   - No sequence has been rendered yet. Zero shutter, which matches Unreal with motion blur off, is the registered default.
3. **Unreal timings are one contended block per configuration**, with visible p95 outliers. They are denominators for planning and gates. Every 2x claim still needs the repeated quiet-window run. UE's CPU fields are recorded in each `summary.json` but not analysed here.
4. **Settle instrument.**
   - Step curves are display-domain PNGs against the +128 capture. They resolve the sky-yaw lag, but not the sun-step lag on this sky-dominated scene.
   - An HDR (EXR) step capture and a sun-dominated variant (for example S4) are needed for the gi.md settle gate.
5. **Unreal side of the new benches.** The glTF exports are prepared, but they have not been imported, placed and cooked into the Unreal project, and no Unreal captures of SV, S4, S5 or S2G exist.
6. **Glossy sweep.**
   - The fixture's roughness levels (0.1, 0.5, 1) and three sun angles were measured on D3D12, and the fp16 bound covers every roughness analytically.
   - A finer roughness sweep (0.05-0.5) would need new fixture materials and was not run.
   - The 4.58° metal at roughness 0.1 has 3 pixels at the capture target's fp16 ceiling (excluded).
7. **Bench design corrections.** The first S4, S5 and S2G renders failed inspection: the panel was not sunlit, the mirror showed no cube, and the metal had no glints. They were regenerated (new identities above) before the retained references (`reference/b`). SV and X1/X2 are unchanged.
8. **Reference Smith form.** The cross-check validates separable Smith, matching Mitsuba. The height-correlated form used on the quarry (the program's) is the same expression as `shaders/scene.wgsl`'s `brdf`, but no independent renderer has checked it. pbrt-v4 uses height-correlated Smith and could close this; it was not installed (no binary release).
9. **Clippy.** `cargo clippy -p renderlab --lib --tests -- -D warnings` reports nothing in this unit's files. It still fails on main's own code with this toolchain (Rust 1.96):
   - `src/virtual_shadow_gpu.rs:203` (`nonminimal_bool`), owned by the shadow hillclimb and left alone;
   - `src/visibility_buffer.rs:1076` (`items_after_test_module`).

   Both predate this branch.
