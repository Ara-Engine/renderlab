## Candidates and claim mapping

These are engineering design-around readings, not legal clearance. No patented fallback is adopted. Primary claim text was checked on 2026-11-08.

All spatial candidates read only current-frame data. A separate single-history stage may follow them. **No spatial candidate reads T1b/TR1's colour, W, age, anchor, moments or masks, or another screen-reprojecting stage's output.** [US 10,600,167 claim 1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10600167B2/en#C1) needs back-projection, same-surface verification and auxiliary information delivered to later filters; it does **not** require variance. Removing variance alone is not the avoidance.

The history is one accumulated value, without a fast history, retained list, temporal moments, colour clamp or lighting-difference detector. Resets are driven by engine events and geometric identity. This follows P0's reading of US 11,847,737 and the history-clamp family; it does not silently assume that claim 1 requires two histories. W1 cache alternatives remain replace-on-update or fixed-weight direction slots, without a counter-and-average.

The joint filter does perform a nearest-direction matching operation. Its reading of US 12,051,145 therefore rests on the other absent combination elements identified in P0 sections 4.8-4.10: every traced secondary ray remains coupled to its own primary hit, and there is no shared projection, frustum or reflector-triangle transform. Do not claim that the matching element itself is absent for this candidate.

## Measurement method

`scripts/m7-d0.py` owns one serial worker. A heavy run starts only with at least 8 GiB available and announces in `E:/renderlab-work/gpu-schedule.md`. Its exclusive `heavy-cpu.lock` is released on exit; process RSS/private memory are sampled against a 1.5 GiB cap. No Cargo target is created. Light preparation reads text/metadata only while the floor is unavailable.

References and floor identities are checked by SHA-256. One fixture load serves every K/filter configuration. The reflection metrics are R0a's unfitted RGB relative RMS, luminance L1, signed energy bias, display RMS/p99 at −14/−10 stops, and its footprint energy, chromatic centroid and eligible edge-width metrics. Every registered population is retained, including complete images, roughness bands, off-screen reflections and thin-feature ROIs. Split-half reference noise is reported without subtracting it from measured error.

Repeated deterministic R0b frames supply the same samples: eight still frames cannot add information to an unchanged input. That fact is not a motion-settle certificate. Motion and event gates require actual sequences and references of the same quantity; a synthetic shift of a still image is not substituted for camera or caster motion.

Estimates count work; they are not GPU timings or comparative performance claims. At 1920×1080, one full-screen pass has 2,073,600 outputs.

| More rays | 2K/4K secondary and visibility/shading work | No spatial-tap work | R0b's eight sun rays per shaded hit remain part of that workload |

For a concrete upper/lower traffic model, count source RGB as 12 B and normal/world/IDs as 32 B, before hit distance and write traffic. A 25-tap unshared fetch model is about 2.28 GB/frame; perfect reuse is tens of MB. Operation counts alone do not choose between those bounds or price SFUs, cache misses and dispatch. A measured Lumen **denoise-only** denominator and an implementation timing are required for D0's ≤½-denoise-share cost gate; whole Lumen/reflection pass time is not that denominator.

**Conditional arithmetic estimate (I):** at an assumed sustained 5 TFLOP/s, not a measured device rate, ~35 scalar operations/tap would price the resolved-signal bilateral arithmetic at ~0.36 ms/1080p frame. ~40 operations/tap would price three à-trous passes at ~1.24 ms. The supplement instead filters **K incident values**: multiply those tap counts and arithmetic proxies by K (K8 bilateral ~2.90 ms, à-trous ~9.95 ms), before SFUs, memory and dispatch. K8 joint search adds 1,600 direction dots/pixel (~16.59 G scalar operations/frame) to up to 200 radiance taps/pixel. These are operation-count proxies, not predicted total GPU milliseconds. Shared geometry/tile reuse or filtering a properly defined integrated incident lobe could change the implementation substantially. **The cost gate remains unverified.**

## Recommendations and fallback order

**R1:** keep B0 undenoised. Separate pixel-footprint aliasing, missing transport/recursive reflection and texture-footprint errors from K-sampling noise before choosing residual filtering. Use current-frame incident quantities and the declared lobe, then final reconstruction with W5 intact. R0b's forever-fixed directions give the same residual each still frame; test complementary deterministic **ray directions** across frames before expecting accumulation to denoise them. That is distinct from the prohibited jitter of filter taps (P0 §4.10). A final-colour diagnostic filter must not become a program-output cache or require albedo export.

**G2:** start from replace-on-update and preserve G1c's W1–W3 contract. The retained W1 comparison above favours it on cold error with identical settled quadrature; adopt direction slots only if their transport-record relighting benefit pays for their cold behaviour and memory in G2's own tests. More rays/finer cells and correct transport come before a pixel blur. G1c already beats the SV sky floor on both paths; its quarry map/standard-program bias and setup/probe cost need their own fixes. Display-PNG improvements cannot certify bounce transport.

**RT shadow grain:** retain RT1c's distinction: the +4-stop witness loses mainly at mixed-footprint boundaries, while fully accepted and partial-shadow populations are better than HRT1. A blur that lowers complete-crop RMS does not demonstrate a shadow-sampling correction. Measure footprint reconstruction and incident visibility separately, retaining narrow/contact and silhouette gates and programmable shading.

**Patent fallback:** D0 does not authorize SVGF or temporal gradients. If every tested unflagged filter misses, record those misses, spend on 2×/4× samples and better/finer caches, then seek the matching patent review only after those alternatives also fail the full static/motion quality gates on both paths. Missing data and an operation-count speed miss do not meet that trigger.
