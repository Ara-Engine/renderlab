For Ara main eb8cc457's Outpost (scene SHA256 f3583f2f9cb02a5fdae641214db4e9e89792f8f3185200708d92a5b760d2e386), the bridge passes density 0.0012/m unchanged and converts the authored 30 m scale height to falloff 1/30 per metre: a horizontal 1 km ray at base height therefore has height-fog optical depth 1.20 (transmittance 0.301, mean free path 833 m). There is an unauthored addition: the bridge inherits VolumetricFog::default().extra_density=0.001/m for the default first 60 m, adding depth 0.060; the analytic height fog starts after that volume, so the height term itself is not counted twice. Enabled aerial perspective adds approximately RGB optical depths [0.01246,0.02022,0.03976] over 1 km at ground level from the authored Rayleigh/Mie scattering plus Mie absorption (ozone zero there); its stored mean transmittance corresponds to scalar depth about 0.0241. Thus combined ground-level depth is about 1.284, transmittance 0.277, subject to the froxel/LUT interpolation and 1 m atmosphere-altitude clamp. Fog coefficients are per metre, sky coefficients per kilometre, with the correct distance conversion; albedo partitions scattering, multiscatter changes in-scattered light, and sun/exposure scale radiance, not extinction. Anisotropy 0.5 can make exactly forward-scattered light six times an isotropic phase, which is brightness rather than a sixfold density multiplier. The authored height-density definition is honoured, but the extra near-field density is not authored; these extinction terms do not explain a sixfold density match, so differing illumination/phase/exposure remains a hypothesis, not an established cause.
