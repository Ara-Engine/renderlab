What blocked lamp measures as
1. Geometric diagnostic: fixed equal-solid-angle directions over the authored spot cone, classify the first hit near the source by fitting primitive versus unrelated caster versus clear. Report numerator/denominator and near-source distance, not a screen-average brightness called visibility. #191 used a first-hit audit within0.8m. Its percentages are attributable evidence, not a new census.
2. Visible consequence: on selected ground/door receiver pixels, use the target light ON minus target light OFF in linear HDR, with RENDERLAB_ABLATE=gi to remove cached-emitter/bounce masking. This is a causal GI ablation retaining direct plus open environment, not a GI-only or purely direct image. Hold other lights/environment/exposure fixed. Count pixels with positive target-light contribution and report luminance distribution over fixed receiver ROIs. The beauty test remains full GI; a glowing bulb without a receiver pool does not count as an unblocked lamp.
3. Shadow guard: inspect cage/bar shadow structure inside the cone and a moving character's shadow across the pool, in held/moving/cut frames. Also compare the fitting's shadows from OTHER lights and an authored-sun control. Do not use only a pixel above a downward spot: that is outside the cone even if its cap stopped casting. An optional near-source occluder fixture would distinguish owner filtering from a ray-start bias; it is a separate test setup, not an edit to main's scene.
No universal brightness threshold is invented before an image/reference exists. Baseline blocker attribution, target-light delta, and before/after crops together distinguish dead source, insufficient exposure and lost legitimate occlusion. #191's corrected-content version is the intended-appearance reference, kept separate from the unchanged-main renderer A/B.

Actual relationships in main
There are21 local lights plus the sun. e_flood0/1/2 are three instances of street_lamp_01, each ONE MeshRenderer containing pole/head, glass and bulb primitives. All cast by default; emissiveMaterials selects bulb/glass appearance, not a no-shadow rule. Their separate light entities e_floodl0/1/2 have positions(73,3.75,79),(119,3.75,79),(73,3.75,105), intensity14000, range34m, inner/outer angles28/48deg. Fitting bases are(73,0.05,79),(119,0.05,79),(73,0.05,105). No parent/owner reference connects those light entities to those mesh entities; naming and spatial placement imply the relationship to a human. The collider's full-pole bounding box is not a mesh-solid test.
#191's asset audit finds the source inside the opaque cap,0.4m above bulb/glass, blocking100% of each cone. Its fix moves each source down0.43m to y3.32 and splits body primitive0(casting), glass1 and bulb2(noncasting). The bars still block10-16% of the corrected cones. Moving the source is a distinct change; a renderer exclusion with the original source cannot reproduce that geometry exactly.
e_seclight uses security_light at(90,6.6,103.6); its separate spot e_seclightl is(90,6.4,103.4), intensity9000, range22m, angles30/55deg. #191 attributes76% blocked cone to bulb63% and glass13%; body0 remains casting, bulb1/glass2 become noncasting, and the source does not move.
The twelve e_gamecache lights share entities/origins with their toolbox meshes and intentionally remain half blocked; campfire, mast beacon, hut light, hangar glow and extraction beacon have different placement intent. Thus even SAME ENTITY plus containment is not a sufficient automatic no-self-shadow contract. The spot intensity field is converted by the bridge to point-equivalent candela; #191's ~48,800 of62,680 blocked nominal light units is not a fresh measurement of received flux.

Existing renderer behavior
Light carries position/travel/candela/range/source radius and a whole-light casts flag, not a caster identity (src/lighting.rs:9). The bridge creates these lights from LightView without an owner (Ara crates/ara-renderlab/src/lighting.rs). Current RT local rays run RECEIVER->sampled source, shortened near the source by max(1e-4*distance,1mm), using SHADOW_CASTER_MASK and terminate-first-hit (shaders/local_light_ray.wgsl:16-46). That endpoint bias does not escape a source buried0.4m in metal. BLAS geometry is OPAQUE (src/hardware_shadow_gpu.rs:268), so the present query loop has no arbitrary-caster rejection callback. A global casts_shadows flag already exists and is filtered from map candidates (src/light_cluster_gpu.rs:630); it disables that caster for every shadowing light. The ordinary non-RT local path samples pooled spot/cube maps, not these hardware rays (src/light_cluster.rs:200-207).

Candidates: cost model and legitimate-shadow risks (no measured milliseconds)
A. Automatically ignore every caster whose AABB contains the source: CPU spatial query per changed light/placement, then per-light exclusions. Cheap to propose, unsafe as a default: containment does not imply ownership or solid enclosure. Rooms, hangars, hollow fittings, toolboxes and overlapping casters are counterexamples. Whole street-lamp exclusion removes its own pole/head/bars as well as its bulb. Reject as a general default.
B. Start rays outside the enclosing box, or stop receiver->source rays at that box entry: arithmetic slab intersection and shorter traversal per ray on RT, with an analogous source-side near-plane treatment on maps. Requires an identified box. It skips ALL geometry in the removed interval, including unrelated nearby casters; a tall pole's box can be much larger than its bulb. It also removes real bars/cap shadowing while attenuation/specular still use the old point. Non-RT cube-face/spot near-plane clipping does not exactly implement a direction-dependent box exit. Reject as a universal bias change; a tiny safe epsilon already exists and is not this fix.
C. Explicit per-light exclusion of a stable caster/primitive set: best controlled renderer-side option if desired. Skip only selected source-owner primitives for that light; retain their sun/other-light shadows and all unrelated blockers. One owner token is O(L) metadata (one u32/light for a single owner, no per-pixel allocation); multiple components need a small indexed list. Association must be supplied or explicitly opted into by the embedding engine: existing main does not encode the floodlight ownership. RT needs candidate filtering, not the current empty proceed loop on opaque triangles: force/mark relevant geometry nonopaque and confirm nonexcluded candidates, or price another traversal organization. Extra candidate control flow can lose an opaque fast path; cost must be measured, not called free. Non-RT can filter the draw/instance/primitive list once per rebuilt light map, with no per-receiver filter; exclusions and generations belong in map cache keys. A separate TLAS per light or one mask bit per owner is not a scalable shortcut (instance ray masks have only8bits). Excluding the whole fitting is cheaper metadata but loses legitimate self-shadow details. Excluding only emissive glass/bulb can address the security lamp yet leaves main's floodlight source buried in opaque cap.
D. Existing global casts_shadows plus #191 content correction: no new query/filter work; potentially less caster work. This remains the clearest reference for Outpost. It requires content/bridge changes, deliberately removes bulb/glass shadows for all lights, and relocates the three source points. Do not present that as a renderer-only fix.

Recommendation
There is no physically correct rule that both leaves an opaque-cap source in place and lets its light pass that cap without an explicit modeling exception. Keep #191's separated primitives and corrected origins as the reference. Do NOT ship automatic AABB exclusion or enlarged ray bias. If a renderer-side alternative is required, first test an opt-in, explicitly associated per-light primitive exclusion against the F0/S0/C0 recipe, preserving unrelated occluders and recording the lost own-fitting shadows; implementation cost is primarily RT candidate filtering and association, not a new visibility pass. It can avoid editing vertex data, but it cannot infer the author's intent from current light/mesh records or promise identical output to the relocated-light reference. No implementation or lease requested by this paper.
