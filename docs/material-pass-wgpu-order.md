# wgpu material-record ordering

The backend uses the shared `program::material_variant` writer and reader, with no packing code of its own. The current record is the two `rgba32uint` words at bindings 75 and 78 defined by `program::MATERIAL_RECORD_BINDINGS` and `MATERIAL_PACKING`. Image allocation, writer storage entries and reader sampled entries derive their count from that shared binding list. A third `rgba32uint` word therefore adds one shared binding and one automatically allocated image; its binding and WGSL producer/consumer remain the shared material implementation's responsibility.

With `RENDERLAB_MATERIAL_PASS=1`, non-film primary frames run camera visibility, material records, receiver sun visibility, GI, local-light visibility, and final shading in that order. RT Interactive frames use the existing separate camera and visibility-buffer sun kernels, including reconstruction's sun estimator when TR1 is active. The material writer is in the early submission, ahead of the late GI/cache preparation. Scene-wide acceleration structures, light clusters, caster maps and atmosphere preparation can precede camera visibility; they do not consume per-pixel receivers.

The record is produced for later receiver passes even when the final output is a view mode. Streaming clears request counters before the writer and reruns the writer when residency changes. Temporal-film accumulation retains its existing fused pipeline. Programs without `material_out`/`material_in` retain their own shading kernels.

This ordering is the foundation for decode-once, not a claim that the current reader has stopped decoding hits. The third word (trace point and epsilon), word 1.w's slot/thin/front metadata, the shared reader's attribute audit, and the sun/GI/light readers must land together before those consumers can stop reading hit/vertex data. That cutover also switches camera visibility to hit-only output: the current camera still publishes the legacy fresh receivers because the current sun kernel consumes them. Opting into this ordering assumes `surface` and `material_out` resolve material inputs independently of this frame's later sun/GI/light-visibility results; the existing game and standard mappings meet that constraint. Their lighting remains in `shade`.

Switch off retains the existing visibility/sun dispatch branch and shader compositions. The new early-order path is default off. Previous #98 timing measurements describe the earlier late writer placement; they must not be treated as timings of this new schedule.
