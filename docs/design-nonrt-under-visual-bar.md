# Non-RT under the visual bar: change the cost structure

Reading-only design study, 2026-11-10; source baseline `9e343a0acfef41c8b4b6fbc50bec15f26b316b62`. No renderer edits, builds, GPU work, new captures or new replay measurements. `docs/p-show2.md` is absent at this baseline. The earlier glossy and stability studies are [PR #4](https://github.com/Ara-Engine/renderlab/pull/4) and [PR #5](https://github.com/Ara-Engine/renderlab/pull/5); their proposals are not assumed shipped.

**Do first:** screen map-based sun visibility with a weight-free geometry rescue; replace static GI acquisition work with cooked geometry records and correct held-pose invalidation; re-screen lower-rate programmable glossy shading on P-DEFECT. Each is independently rejectable. Keep native geometry/contact information, explicit cache validity and current-frame disocclusion recovery; do not borrow screen-space omissions or longer lag as the price of speed.

## Evidence labels and the objective

- **M:** measured in a named repo report, not reproduced here. Its hardware, scene, source identity, settling, profile and contention qualifications remain attached.
- **S:** reported by a linked primary source. **C** is the same evidence category for source-reported constants/behavior in the pinned repository implementation, not a GPU measurement.
- **E:** this study's estimate, inference, arithmetic forecast, budget allocation or proposed experiment. Every forecast below is E, even when anchored in measured input. Formula constants are mathematical definitions; dates, versions, patent/section/line identifiers are locators.

[AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md)'s governing test is the final visible image, still and in motion: keep faster changes that are not visibly worse. Byte equality is strong evidence for a pure optimization, not a reason to exclude reconstructed visibility, precomputation, temporally integrated samples or filtered maps. The minimum remains Unreal-quality; a gain that reveals leaks, contact loss, motion smear or extra flicker fails. The desired performance ratio does not override that test.

**M:** [P-YARD2](p-yard.md#full-table-all-presets), matched native 1080p quarry, RTX 4070 SUPER / NVIDIA 617.14, Renderlab `71ea355`, UE 5.7.4:

| State | Renderlab non-RT GPU ms (M) | Unreal SW Lumen Epic GPU ms (M) | Required saving to become faster (E arithmetic) | Frame budget for 3× UE speed (E arithmetic) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Still | 8.65 | 4.02 | greater than `8.65−4.02 = 4.63 ms` | `4.02/3 = 1.34 ms` |
| Moving | 10.57 | 4.12 | greater than `10.57−4.12 = 6.45 ms` | `4.12/3 = 1.37 ms` |
| Cut | 43.11 | 4.11 | greater than `43.11−4.11 = 39.00 ms` | `4.11/3 = 1.37 ms` |

**E arithmetic:** present slowdowns are `8.65/4.02 = 2.15×`, `10.57/4.12 = 2.57×`, and `43.11/4.11 = 10.49×`. Reaching the aspirational budgets means about **6.46× / 7.70× / 31.47×** acceleration respectively, not a universal sevenfold cut-frame improvement. Beat Epic first, then lower the next remaining cost. The cut is an architectural problem as well as a sampling problem.

**M:** P-YARD's reference error is better than every Unreal preset, but non-RT flicker **4.44** loses to SW Epic **3.69**. Flat-wall grain and the RT path's stray bright edge/ground pixels are observed separately in S4. The new permission to approximate does not turn those weaknesses into an acceptable trade.

### Current implementation boundaries (C)

- `VirtualLookup::Surface` caches consensus-certified counts but walks geometry on misses. `Map` is a selectable light-space-only alternative; `Hybrid` rescues ambiguous map decisions with the **old exact march**, so it is not the new patent-avoiding hybrid proposed below ([virtual_shadow_gpu.rs:177–207](https://github.com/Ara-Engine/renderlab/blob/9e343a0acfef41c8b4b6fbc50bec15f26b316b62/src/virtual_shadow_gpu.rs#L177)). S4 uses a screen receiver queue but tests world geometry; that is not screen-space-only visibility.
- S4 selection depends on batch count, image size, sun disk and eligible geometry; its plane records and boolean tests deliberately avoid barycentric depth reconstruction ([sunvis_s4.rs:14–43](https://github.com/Ara-Engine/renderlab/blob/9e343a0acfef41c8b4b6fbc50bec15f26b316b62/src/sunvis_s4.rs#L14), [sunvis_s4.wgsl:1–24](https://github.com/Ara-Engine/renderlab/blob/9e343a0acfef41c8b4b6fbc50bec15f26b316b62/shaders/sunvis_s4.wgsl#L1)). Quarry's full-detail memory/coverage is not solved by selecting S4 unconditionally.
- GI still treats a held skin as deforming on software frames, explicitly scaffolding ([gi_cache.rs:2284–2295](https://github.com/Ara-Engine/renderlab/blob/9e343a0acfef41c8b4b6fbc50bec15f26b316b62/src/gi_cache.rs#L2284)). The gather cap is now **C 32,768 cells/frame on both paths**, not the old **8,192** used by the earlier drain forecasts. [P-DEFECT §3](p-defect.md#3-non-rt-blotches-one-gather-budget) improved matched-scene fill, but did not verify Showcase with the scaffolding removed.
- `Engine::cut()` already keeps world GI cells; it resets TR1/fog and marks a camera cut. Newly seen cells cause acquisition work on their own ([engine/mod.rs:1031–1040](https://github.com/Ara-Engine/renderlab/blob/9e343a0acfef41c8b4b6fbc50bec15f26b316b62/src/engine/mod.rs#L1031)). “Stop clearing GI on every camera cut” is therefore not a new fix. Cooked/streamed visibility targets *new* cells and stale regions.
- R1n rays traverse per-mesh binary trees and a flat instance list, then shade hits and their shadows inline ([reflections_software.wgsl](../shaders/reflections_software.wgsl), [reflections.wgsl](../shaders/reflections.wgsl)). The quarry has negligible traced glossy opportunity under coverage/roughness rules; large/skinned software-reflection coverage remains incomplete. Mixed-program reflection hits currently use a receiver-program fallback, not correct hit-program dispatch ([program.rs:545–553](https://github.com/Ara-Engine/renderlab/blob/9e343a0acfef41c8b4b6fbc50bec15f26b316b62/src/program.rs#L545)). Cheaper reflections must preserve the intended own-hit-program contract, not make that fallback the design.

## 2. What the earlier round excluded, and what changes now

The previous premise needs a distinction: **it did not exclude every cache or precomputation**. Surface verdict caching already shipped; G-A explicitly proposed cooked geometry-only visibility/hit keys; TR1 already integrated samples. What it excluded was approximating/reconstructing their values and freely changing sample rate. Those exclusions now become experiments with visual gates. Off-screen completeness, shader programmability, patent constraints and real speed failures remain binding.

## 4. Patent position: the baseline is not a safe harbor

Engineering claim reading, not legal clearance. [P0](m7-p0-claims.md#3-independent-claim-elements) and [P-VIA, including its divisional appendix](patent-via-8471853.md#divisional-us-8605088) are mandatory element charts. Patent numbers/status below are reported by those records and linked primary text, not new USPTO maintenance certification.

**The shipping sun march is flagged.** [US8471853B2 claim 1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8471853B2/en#C1) requires front-face geometry extraction, vertex-coordinate consistency/weight testing, reconstructed occluder depth and a shadow determination. [US8605088B2 claim 1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8605088B2/en#C1) explicitly combines geometry-shadow-map lookup, inverse-function coordinate weights, weighted vertex-z depth and comparison, with vertices stored in the map. Both granted claim sections were read; the divisional was retrieved through its application's “Other versions” link. Abstracts omit important narrowing limitations.

P-VIA maps the shipping march's subareas, location walk and weighted height onto those computations and records unresolved pixel/storage claim-construction questions: **AT RISK**, not adjudicated infringement. It reports both maintained/active with paid installments; do not assume expiry/lapse. Caching the answer does not remove the method used on a miss. Cut-only S4 leaves the route reachable on other frames.

The new shadow candidate deliberately drops triangle-coordinate consistency and reconstructed light-view depth: depth texture comparisons for the approximate part; plane/edge-side boolean segment occupancy for geometry rescue. Its rescue must not call the old barycentric/height helper merely because the return type is boolean. Full family avoidance requires no legacy march fallback in that retained configuration, including on wide suns and ineligible meshes. Until geometry coverage and replacement are complete, report the remaining baseline exposure; do not describe an opt-in experiment as repository-wide clearance.

## 5. Ranked plan: standalone steps and cheap rejection

Ranking is by likely leverage and value of the next screen. **All savings in this section are E**. Measurements used as inputs are marked M. Savings overlap: never add a ray-rate reduction to the full old traversal saving, or count GI lookup both in shading and GI-pass totals. Keep one owner for a runtime subsystem if later implementations run in parallel.

### Step 1 — Map-based sun visibility with selective world-geometry rescue

**Build:** first use existing Map captures/settings as a diagnostic, then replace its ambiguous/near-contact rescue with S4-style **boolean BVH** queries instead of the legacy march. Trace world casters, including off-screen occluders; keep the map full resolution initially. Only after that passes, screen a fixed lower-rate soft-visibility lattice with full-resolution receiver/normal/depth guides and full-rate contact/disocclusion rescue.

This is not “turn on Map and accept its errors.” [N8k](non-rt-n8k.md) already measured the map-only limit: **M** quarry moving **0.94–0.96 ms**, Exact **1.95 ms**, Hybrid **1.51–1.53 ms**; Map darkened too many lit receivers, Hybrid beat VSM on the tested measures but missed the old speed target. Near relief below a texel cannot be inferred from a map plane. Moving casters also made plane preprocessing expensive. Those are concrete failure cases to preserve in the new screen.

**E savings arithmetic:** let `B` be map/filter cost, `q` rescued fraction, `C` the full geometry-query cost and `O` rescue/dispatch overhead: `new = B + q×C + O`. With **E** `B=0.60 ms`, `q=0.10`, `O=0.60 ms`, and **M P-S4C game** full-geometry costs **8.08 / 10.55 / 8.41 ms** as illustrative still/moving/selected-cut inputs, new cost is **2.01 / 2.26 / 2.04 ms**. Against **M** march/chosen costs **2.83 / 5.57 / 8.41**, savings are **0.82 / 3.31 / 6.37 ms**. This linear rescue model is unverified: a tiny query queue can retain a large latency floor and a different cone distribution. Do not transfer these game numbers to the quarry's **M 43.11 ms** cut.

**E lower-rate extension:** for per-receiver cost `C`, fixed quarter-rate evaluation plus full-rate rescue on fraction `q` yields work fraction `r = 0.25 + 0.75q`. At assumed `q=0.10`, `r=0.325`; net saving `0.675C − reconstruction overhead`. This is a possible second saving *on the remaining bill*, not additional savings from the old bill. Geometry/raster/page creation does not scale with this fraction.

**Visual risk / motion judgment:** PCF/PCSS-like errors include acne, detached contacts, wrong multi-blocker penumbrae, warped grazing shadows and thin-pole disappearance. Compare wide and original sun, S1/S2/S3, quarry gravel/crevices, sliding door, and camera cuts at normal scale and in strips. Rescue based only on a smooth-looking receiver misses a thin distant caster; classify with light-space depth variation/coverage and current geometry, not only image colour. Do not hide bias with longer history. Final image must retain Renderlab's advantage over UE and its own old image, including off-screen casters.

**Patent:** §4's VIA breaks for both map and rescue, no legacy fallback in the retained replacement; keep S4 gather constraints. No temporal statistics/moments or jittered reconstruction taps. Geometry queue ordering may change without demanding byte identity, but the actual predicate/dataflow must be re-charted.

**Cheap pre-build gate:** compare retained Map/Hybrid/Surface frames at the exact worst old lit-darkened/contact pixels; CPU-replay map classifications and boolean rescue on their receiver rays. Count needed rescue pixels and candidate pairs, including wide suns/mesh boundaries. Reject if `B+qC+O` cannot beat the current matched pass or if rescuing the observed error needs most pixels. Existing Hybrid is a diagnostic only; its old-march fallback is not promoted. If buffers were deleted, take one future small raw receiver/map capture on a GPU host rather than build a new full renderer first.

**Coverage/memory stop:** current S4 serves game-sized eligible geometry. The quarry's coarse GI BVH is not full-detail contact visibility. **E arithmetic:** naive plane records for all **M P-YARD 13,034,903** quarry triangles at **C 96 bytes/triangle** already cost about **1.25 GB before BVH nodes**; not an acceptable invisible allocation. Reject that representation or partition/compress/reuse cooked near geometry before building the replacement. Unknown geometry uses a complete world-space alternative during development, with remaining march exposure explicitly recorded; it does not earn a cleared/default claim.

### Step 2 — Geometry-only GI acquisition and correct invalidation

**Build:** first re-screen removing software GI's false held-skin deformation using the already merged gather-budget fix. Independently cook/stream static world-cell query/visibility records and compact gather hit references for a bounded scene; on claim, reuse valid geometry records and evaluate all shading, emission and changed lighting at runtime. Dynamic occluders/changed topology invalidate or rebuild affected geometry records, rather than merely changing a colour blend.

This step does not depend on Step 1. It revives **G-A**, which was already permitted, with packed precision/approximate geometry allowed by the visual bar. It does not require baking every reachable world cell up front. Scene geometry versions, LOD identity, instance transforms and deformed vertices are part of validity. A baked hit key is not a lifetime pointer into an evicted hash slot; resolve stable world/material identities and generation checks. Cooked transport must not omit moving objects or off-screen geometry. A sun or material change relights; it must not force recooking static geometry visibility unnecessarily.

**E savings arithmetic:** P-SHOW's residual ablations suggest a large duplicate-work opportunity: **M** `17.82 − 3.53 = 14.29 ms` residual difference between baseline and the GI-included held-pose experiment, but conditions/shading differ. Use **E 8–12 ms** as a conservative *screening opportunity*, not a current measured saving; no improvement is guaranteed after current budget changes. On scenes without held skins this part saves **E approximately zero**.

For cooked acquisition, historical **M P-GI** gather cost `85.4/40 = 2.135 ms/frame` is an arithmetic average over an old drain. If **E** `75%` of rays can become valid static hit-record reads and those reads cost `0.30 ms/frame`, prospective saving is `2.135×0.75−0.30 = 1.30 ms/drain frame`. Cell setup/visibility costs can also fall but must be separately measured; do not count the **8–12 ms** churn opportunity again. **E 0.2–0.6 ms** steady new-cell acquisition saving is a test budget, while fully settled static scenes may gain almost nothing beyond probe/invalidation reductions. Current larger gather cap changes the distribution of work, not the need for a first-visible correct value.

**Visual risk / motion judgment:** stale occlusion, missing skinned blockers, moving-door bounce that persists, cold-cell flashes, or delayed sun/material edits. Compare first visible frame after teleport, held-pose Showcase plinths, real moving Fox, light colour/intensity step and returning to a visited room. A longer settling time is a visible regression even if per-frame drain cost falls. Reference quality and TR1 flicker must survive; no frozen/no-bounce fallback passes merely because it is smooth.

**Patent:** world cubic geometry records, replace/fixed-direction values, deterministic lookup and fixed refresh; no hash sum+counter average, ranked update locations, RIS or per-triangle shaded grids. Shade user `surface`/`scatter`/`emission` at runtime. Do not turn geometry acquisition into a lighting-value-ranked irradiance cache schedule.

**Cheap pre-build gates:** the held-pose condition is already an isolated code seam; first inspect the existing P-DEFECT late-hold vs early-fill evidence and compare one future Showcase pair before declaring its switch retained. For the cook, count unique static geometry hit references, invalidation reach and packed bytes over a saved bounded camera route. CPU-replay unpacked visibility/hit identity and shade-change cases; reject if static reuse is low, storage exceeds the streaming budget, or dynamic changes reach most records. Cross-process byte equality is evidence, not a veto on a visibly equivalent packed result.

### Step 3 — Lower-rate programmable glossy reflection shading

**Build:** re-screen full-receiver-rate glossy count reduction on P-DEFECT, with the baseline cone/texture/environment footprint held fixed; mirrors retain their full-rate ray. If motion fails, split ray/hit records from receiver shading, dispatch hits by their **own** shader program and reconstruct the incident lobe before final-colour TR1. Sparse receiver rates are a later screen, not a reason to use the wrong hit shader or surface-motion history.

**E savings arithmetic:** **M P-SHOW** reflection-attributable shading difference is `20.78−2.18 = 18.60 ms`. For affected cost fraction **E `f=0.6–0.8`** and a **E** `16→4` count (`r=0.25`), saving is `18.60×f×0.75 = 8.37–11.16 ms` before any new reconstruction pass. For **E 0.7–1.5 ms** dispatch/reconstruction overhead, net is **6.87–10.46 ms** if reconstruction is actually required. Mixed counts and hits change the model. On the quarry, expect **E near-zero ray saving**; do not use this to close its GPU budget.

**Visual risk / motion judgment:** renewed white specks, lost reflected-sun glow, broadened highlights, missing thin targets, temporally lagging reflections. Current hit regularization widens as count falls unless separated from sampling rate; a wider cone is not proof of unchanged quality. Custom BRDFs bypassing engine regularization require their own opt-in/full-rate behavior. Moving curved mirrors and reflected targets invalidate simple mean-distance reprojection. See the full [glossy study](https://github.com/Ara-Engine/renderlab/pull/4) for this boundary.

**Patent:** ordinary own-pixel strata first; no reservoirs or common projection. Later fixed-tap current incident reconstruction must follow §4's temporal-flow constraints; neighbor reassignment is a thinner reflection-patent boundary. Separate hit-program lists retain arbitrary user code; no Lumen-style cached final material output.

**Cheap pre-build gate:** replay ray-weighted lobe/count and hit-frequency captures, not texture roughness percentages. Subsampling saved strata can screen noise but is not an exact replay of a new pattern. Existing matched turntable/spinning-crate sequence is the smallest future GPU screen if ray records were deleted. Reject at the first visibly worse speck/glow/motion crop. Do not repeat the fade-band-only variant that measured within noise.

### Step 4 — Lower-rate GI lookup with full-resolution material shading

**Build:** move expensive world-cache incident lookup out of the inlined material kernel. Query a fixed coarse receiver lattice and reconstruct **incident coefficients** using full-resolution plane/side/normal guides; use a compact diffuse/rough-specular representation where the program opts in, and fresh full-rate queries at contacts, thin geometry and disocclusions. Material textures, user `shade` and direct-light visibility stay at their required rate.

This does not depend on cooked visibility: it can consume today's WRC unchanged. A screen-aligned query lattice is not screen-colour transport: each queried value includes off-screen world geometry. Returning large coefficient arrays can erase bandwidth savings; initially reconstruct only declared consumed quantities and keep arbitrary directional programs on the direct lookup. No lighting-model shortcut is forced on user code.

**E savings arithmetic:** from historical **M approximately `0.80 ms`** lookup, **E** quarter-rate + rescue fraction `q=0.10` leaves `r=0.325`. With **E `0.10–0.20 ms`** reconstruction/guide traffic, net saving `0.80×0.675−(0.10–0.20) = 0.34–0.44 ms/frame`. A separate-kernel occupancy gain is possible but unmeasured, so not added. This cannot by itself pay the **E 6.45 ms** moving deficit. Probe reuse may remove part of historical **M 0.45 ms** probe work, but count its own generation-check/seen-mark traffic separately.

**Visual risk / motion judgment:** crevice brightening, boundary light leaks, over-smoothed indirect shadows, block-shaped flicker when the rescue mask changes. Inspect slow/fast pans over gravel, indoor corners, bright doorway reveal and non-GGX authored materials. The existing G3 filter is the control; a generic additional blur that loses contact contrast fails. Reconstruct current input before temporal history; do not borrow a neighbor's stale full-colour history to make the coarse grid disappear.

**Patent:** fixed spatial taps, no downstream TR1 age/rejection, no variance/fast histories, no ranked cache refresh or RIS. Geometry-guided rescue is not a sample-count/disocclusion switch from per-pixel lighting to a separate cache source: both rates query the same WRC incident field. Re-chart if a different lighting source or adaptive temporal denoiser is introduced.

**Cheap pre-build gate:** CPU-mask retained full-rate incident/geometry buffers to the candidate lattice and reconstruct; compare reference contact/detail/flicker witnesses. Count full-rate rescue and bytes of coefficient output. Reject if most coarse samples fail surface compatibility, if reconstructed coefficients move as many bytes as the old records, or if costs cannot exceed dispatch overhead. P-GIE's maxima are not a reason to reject this different operation, nor evidence it wins.

### Step 5 — Cheaper world visibility representations where the image tolerates them

**Build:** use cone-sized cluster cuts, compact BVHs or static distance fields for broad GI/glossy visibility, retaining exact near geometry for thin contacts and sharp reflections. This is a world-space alternative to both SSR and a full-detail per-ray memory bill; it can stand alone with the existing sampler.

**E savings arithmetic:** if the remaining software traversal bill is `C`, fraction `f` eligible, traversal speedup `s`, and added fallback/dispatch cost `O`, saving is `C×f×(1−1/s)−O`. Illustrative **E** `C=4 ms`, `f=0.75`, `s=2`, `O=0.30 ms` yields **1.20 ms**. This is a hypothetical remaining bill, not a measured quarry pass. After Step 3 fewer rays remain, so price it again. A proxy that makes shader hits cheap by discarding UV/program identity is not equivalent.

**Visual risk / motion judgment:** SDF thin-wall leaks, hidden cavities, proxy bias, silhouette/LOD pops and specular mismatch. Trace off-screen thin poles, open meshes and grazing relief; inspect entry/exit of LOD ranges in motion, not just a static blurred reflection. Exact near rescue needs the *relevant* occluders, not merely a fixed distance that misses a far thin caster. A full-detail scene cannot be replaced globally by Lumen's known leaking representation under this bar.

**Patent:** P0 W8 global-first independent rays, no per-cell primary-hit registration/local launches, no common reflection projection. No irradiance-ranking cache/RIS combination. Distance-field generation and compressed-BVH portfolios still need a focused search before selecting a concrete implementation.

**Cheap pre-build gate:** CPU trace recorded receiver cones/rays against full and candidate representations; measure missed occluders, projected hit/normal/material displacement and visited bytes. Use the visual footprint to interpret differences rather than byte equality. Reject if only a large rescue fraction makes it correct, or if storage/build/streaming cost exceeds the chosen device budget.

### Step 6 — Small remaining work and scheduling, after the large bills

**Build:** compose non-reflecting kernels when tracing is disabled; reduce redundant environment fetches/record traffic where a lower precision/filtered lookup is visibly equivalent. Reconsider bounded cooperative GI reads with an overflow path only if new *shading* key counts support it. Scheduling changes are a separate service-time experiment.

**E savings arithmetic:** quarry's old reflection-codegen increment was **M 0.30–0.52 ms**, so that is a historical opportunity ceiling, not a guaranteed current recovery. A **E `0.20–0.40 ms`** retained reduction in this residual bill would be useful near parity; count it only after a matched pair. **M P-NRT3b** two-submit split cut non-RT game moving **service `11.46→11.27 ms`**, while a separate trace's inter-frame gap fell **`1.852→1.253 ms`** and GPU span did not improve. It remains unmerged after an RT early-still median miss. Thus scheduling contributes **E zero** to the P-YARD GPU-saving ledger until a GPU-span win is actually measured.

**Visual risk / motion judgment:** precision bands, changed hit tie order or cross-frame resource hazards. Inspect low-light gradients, extreme HDR and stable exposure sweeps; scheduling must preserve scene/data ownership and all current-frame updates. The visual bar permits rounding differences; it does not permit a stale frame. Optional reduced internal resolution is a later independent screen: TR1 Quality scales axes by **C 2/3**, or **E 4/9** pixel work, but geometry/caches/resolve do not all scale that way, and prior reduced-resolution fine specular stepping remains a quality risk. No whole-frame linear speedup is claimed.

**Patent:** no new protected sampling/filter combinations for plain composition/scheduling. If precision work changes temporal/filter/cache logic, §4 applies. Do not reintroduce jittered spatial taps or auxiliaries passed out of TR1 as a side effect.

**Cheap pre-build gate:** count actual no-ray frames, shader/environment requests and coefficient bandwidth from existing manifests/profiles. For cooperation, estimate mean *and overflow tail* of live shading keys, not only probe unions; reject if spills and occupancy eat the load saving. For scheduling, reuse P-NRT3b's distribution and audit resource writes before any retest, rather than resurrecting its old WGSL-blocker diagnosis.

## 7. Decision order and visible acceptance

**The first three steps are 1, 2 and 3.** The first attacks both the quarry's moving/cut cost and the existing sun patent exposure. The second attacks first-visible/cut work and Showcase's false invalidation; the held-pose check is a small early substep, not a new bake prerequisite. The third attacks Showcase's biggest remaining shading bill, and tests whether the merged speck fix changes the earlier rejection. All can be retained or rejected independently; Step 4 is the next native quarry lever if reflection savings cannot apply there.

Reject a method at the cheapest screen that demonstrates insufficient work savings or visible loss. For accepted screens, use one warm paired process where possible, reset the relevant caches for replay identity, and log the exact source/content/quality state. No full campaign is needed for an obviously worse crop. Reuse prior reference frames only after matching content and lighting identity.

**E acceptance set for a GPU host later:** matched P-YARD still/walk/cut/recovery; S4 door and S5 off-screen mirror target; old shadow Map failure pixels; wide-sun penumbrae and gravel contacts; Showcase plinths/turntable/mannequins held and animated; moving thin casters, light steps, disocclusion and return-to-room. Judge full final images and short motion strips at normal display scale, with diagnostic enlarged crops to locate causes. Preserve shadow anchoring/width, indirect contact contrast, reflected objects/highlight shape and texture detail. Measure flicker, light response and ghost extent as well as reference error, never only mean RMS or byte differences.

A lower-rate method may use image geometry to reconstruct complete world-space lighting. **SSR / screen-colour GI remain last resort**: expected visual costs are absent off-screen reflectors, history-dependent lighting, holes/lag after disocclusion and camera-dependent transport. These are exactly the failures the world-geometry path is meant to avoid. If the first experiments fail, widen the world-space representation/visibility options before accepting those costs. A screen-only configuration below the image floor is not a default or a successful speed result.

## 8. What reading cannot determine

- The current P-YARD per-pass split, the actual size of the shadow rescue set, and the present static GI acquisition share. Historical profiles span different sample budgets, bases, cache ages and scenes. No sum of them proves a current speedup.
- Whether the already merged gather fix makes Showcase clean with held-skin GI scaffolding removed; whether fixed-footprint fewer rays pass its motion/glow/speck screen; whether map errors remain visibly worse after boolean rescue. No retained P-SHOW2 exists here.
- Packed static-record size/streaming latency, dynamic invalidation reach, software visibility throughput on other GPUs, and memory fit for full quarry geometry. Cooking moves work to acquisition; it must be priced and versioned, not called free runtime work for arbitrary dynamic content.
- Whether the strong-case steady/moving allocations can be realized, and the much harder cut target. CPU service, GPU span, per-pass time and contention are different quantities. No GPU or CPU transport replay ran in this study; Windows raw evidence paths were not accessed.
- Complete patent clearance, including foreign counterparts, equivalents and broader filtering/cache portfolios. The baseline march's VIA flag remains open until a complete retained replacement removes its reachable path. A visually acceptable approximation can be an engineering design-around; its name alone never proves one.
