## 2. What G3 builds

### 2.1 The cell-space filter (`IndirectSettings::filter` bits 0 and 1)

One compute pass (`gi_filter`, [gi_cache.wgsl](../shaders/gi_cache.wgsl)) after finish and integrate, before light visibility and cell shading:
- **Sources.** Each cell's own values stay where the kernels compute them, now mirrored into a source buffer (`gi_raw`, hot words 1-22, 88 bytes a slot) by `gi_store_sh` (sky) and `gi_gather` (bounce). Shading never reads it.
- **Which cells.** Every cell whose sky or bounce changed this frame is already in the cell shading queue (finish, gathers, relights; integrate now queues its cells too while the filter is on). Each queue entry rewrites itself and its eight neighbours, since their filtered values read it.
- **The filter.** A cell's value is the weighted mean of the sources of itself and the eight cells around it in its bin's tangent plane at its level. Each neighbour is found one edge along the tangents from the cell's query point, moved along the bin's axis onto the query plane, so a sloping surface's neighbours in the next layer are found.
- **Weights**, all fixed by geometry: a tent (1 at the centre, `filter_edge` e = 0.5 on the edges, e² on the corners), times `smoothstep(0.5, 0.9, n_m·n)` on the query normals, times `clamp(1.5 − 3 |(q_m − q)·n| / edge, 0, 1)`, the distance from the query plane. Cells across a crease, a step or another layer weigh nothing.
- **What it replaces.** The hot words that shading, ES1's specular and cell shading read. The sources are never overwritten by filtered values, so nothing is filtered twice and no value blends with an earlier one.
- **Bounce state.** Whether a cell has been gathered is now its bounce source's word (zeroed when its slot is claimed). Only gathered neighbours weigh in for bounce; `GI_BOUNCE_NONE` neighbours weigh in as zero.
- **Determinism.** Every thread that rewrites a cell in a frame computes the same words from the same sources, and the pass writes no source, so the result does not depend on thread order.

Why it works (I): each cell's 64 fixed directions are rotated by its key, so neighbouring cells' quadrature errors are independent. Where light enters through a narrow opening, a handful of directions carry most of it, and that error showed as cell-sized mottle. The tent's nine cells average about seven cells' worth of independent directions.

### 2.2 Contrast refine (bit 2)

A cell much darker than an agreeing neighbour is marked split in its hot record. Agreeing means a neighbour whose normal and plane factors multiply to at least 0.5. Much darker means the cell's openness is below a quarter of the neighbour's while the neighbour is more than a quarter open; openness is its sky irradiance at its query normal over the open sky's. Its pixels then read the next finer level (GI_REFINE), as G1c's two-layer cells are, and the probe requests those cells.
- This is G2's diagnosis (section 4 of m7-g2.md): a mouth cell's query point sits a few centimetres inside the gap, and its darkness was spread over the open pixels at the mouth. G2b measured that 2 px cells everywhere fix it but speckle flat surfaces and need a 448 MiB table. Contrast refine puts 2 px cells only on such visibility edges.
- The rule reads only the sky sources, which depend on visibility and the environment, so it is stable once a cell and its neighbours are traced. It is recomputed whenever the filter rewrites the cell.
- The flag shares the hot record's split half with G1c's flag; the cold `GI_SPLIT` state stays the probe lines' own.

### 2.3 Bounce fill (bit 3)

A cell that is valid but not gathered yet takes its gathered neighbours' filtered bounce, instead of the lookup filling its share from the coarser level (G2c). Non-RT's gather cap leaves cells waiting for many frames after a cut, and those cells showed as cell-sized dark specks on SV's flat panels while the queue drained (G2b section 4). A cell with no gathered neighbour keeps "not gathered yet", and G2c's coarser fill handles it as before.

## 8. Not built (recorded in the gaps)

- **Half-resolution lookup.** A separate pass would write each half-resolution receiver's blended lookup (9 sky + 4 bounce RGB coefficients, 78 bytes in halves), and shading would read four of them per pixel for a geometry-aware upsample. That is about 312 bytes a pixel against the present lookup's four 96-byte corner records, plus a pass and about 40 MB of buffer at 1080p (I). The registers it would free from shading may still make it pay; it needs its own unit and an Nsight trace.
- **Direct light at gather hits** for the hollow's bias (section 3.2).

## 9. Claim notes (m7-p0-claims.md)

- **W1** (no counter, no running mean). The filter is a fixed-weight spatial mean of the current sources of nine cells, recomputed from those sources whenever one changes. No field counts samples, nothing is accumulated across updates, and a filtered value is never a source. Bounce fill reads the same sources.
- **W2** (no ranking). The filter decides no rays: it runs after the frame's rays, over the cells whose values changed. Contrast refine is a fixed per-cell rule on visibility against geometric neighbours, like G1c's two-layer split. It decides which finer cells pixels read, never which rays an existing cell casts. No cell is ordered against others or against a budget.
- **W3** (fixed weights, no jitter, fixed query point). Tent, normal and plane weights are fixed by geometry and the setting. No tap is jittered; query points are unchanged.
- **US 11,688,042** (separable filters over several passes, filter directions). The filter is one non-separable 3x3 pass at each cell's level. Its size is fixed by the setting, not derived from ray-traced data (US 12,423,782 element (b); its jitter (a) is absent too).
- **US 10,600,167** (a temporal filter's auxiliary data passed to later filters). The filter reads cache cells only, no TR1 or reprojected data (W5, W6 unchanged).
